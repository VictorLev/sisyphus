        Progress

        Lv 1

         

        XP comes from time and distance ridden, plus each completed Push — quantities that never change, so raising your FTP can never cost you a level.

        Rider

        FTP is used to show workout targets as a percentage of threshold. Weight and max HR are stored for later use (power-to-weight, heart-rate zones) and change nothing today.

        SETTINGS

        The knobs behind the ride.

        Virtual Gears

        Gears are spread evenly between min and max. The trainer accepts 0–20; lower the max if the top gears feel too hard. Grade adds resistance on top of your gear — raise the per-grade figure to make climbs bite harder.

        Ride

        Smoothing affects the live watts display only — recorded samples are always raw. Sample interval sets how often a data point is written to the ride log.
