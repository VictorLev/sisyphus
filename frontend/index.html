                        FTP History

                          Every FTP you've had: from an FTP Test, or saved above under Rider.

                          FTP Test

                          Your FTP (functional threshold power) is roughly the highest power you could hold for an hour. Sisyphus uses it for your power zones and to turn the library's % targets into watts, so an accurate FTP makes every workout the right difficulty.

                          63 minutes. Connect the trainer first, with the button at the top of the page.

                          The FTP test, part by part
                          WhenPartWhat to do

                          Pacing the 20 minutes

                          • Start at about your current FTP for the first 5 minutes, then raise it if you feel good. Starting too hard is the most common way to ruin the test.
                          • Aim for an even effort and keep the last 3 minutes for whatever you have left.
                          • The test runs in virtual gears (or your bike's gears, if that's your setting): the trainer doesn't hold a wattage for you, so shift with + and − or the on-screen buttons to set the pace.

                          Your result

                          Your new FTP is 95% of your average power over the best 20 minutes: a 20-minute effort is a little above what you could hold for an hour. After the ride you see the estimate next to your current FTP and choose whether to update it; nothing changes on its own. If the ride ends before a full 20 minutes, there's no result.

                          Before you start

                          Ride it rested (no hard ride the day before), with a fan and water nearby.

                          SETTINGS

                          The knobs behind the ride.

                          Trainer

                          Gears are spread evenly between min and max. The trainer accepts 0–20; lower the max if the top gears feel too hard.

                          Phone remote

                          Shift from your phone on the handlebars: open this address on it and log in. It shows big buttons, your gear and your numbers during a ride.

                          /remote

                          A workout's climbs add this much resistance for each 1% of grade; raise it to make them bite harder.

                          ERG: the trainer holds each segment's target watts, whatever your gear or cadence. Workouts only; free rides, routes and FTP tests always use your gears. You can also switch it for one workout when you pick it on Home.

                          Ride

                          Speed while riding comes from your watts, the grade, and your weight (Profile) plus the bike's, as it would on the road: heavier is slower uphill and slower to get going.

                          Smoothing affects the live watts display only — recorded samples are always raw. Sample interval sets how often a data point is written to the ride log.

                          Display

                          Scenery

                          The videos are the same for everyone, chosen by the admin. Random never repeats last ride's video; In order plays the next one each ride. Videos start muted; unmute in the player. To switch mid-ride, use the picker on the ride screen.

                          Strava

                          Adds a box to each ride's page in the Chronicle: download the ride as a .tcx file, then upload it on Strava's site. Nothing is sent to Strava by Sisyphus.
